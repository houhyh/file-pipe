# Fictional procurement workflow fixture

Author: project maintainers. Purpose: deterministic software tests, not real-world evidence. License: CC0-1.0.

Before payment, compare the purchase order, goods receipt and supplier invoice. Record short delivery as an exception and reconcile the invoice with received quantities.

Procurement confirms the agreed unit price and contract payment terms. Operations checks receipt quantities and coordinates delivery exceptions. Finance approves the reconciled payable balance.

Keep the purchase order, receipt record, invoice, reconciliation and approval record together so each settlement decision has supporting records.
