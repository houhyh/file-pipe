# Fictional payment policy fixture

Author: project maintainers. Purpose: deterministic software tests, not a real institution policy. License: CC0-1.0.

The payable amount equals received quantity multiplied by agreed unit price. Hold the invoiced value of unreceived goods pending resolution; do not release that balance for payment.

Calculate the due date from invoice date and the contract payment days. Any change to payment terms must state its effective date and identify the affected unpaid invoice.

Retain the original agreement when an amendment changes payment terms. Apply each rule only within its stated effective period and preserve the old reconciliation as a historical version.
